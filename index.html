Benvenuti!

I’m Sebastian, and I made this portfolio site as part of my application for Residenza Lago Scuro. Grazie mille for your time and consideration!

About Me

I was born in California, USA, and raised in Minneapolis, MN. Throughout my life, I’ve bounced between urban and rural environments. My family often went camping while I was growing up, and as a young adult, I worked for the Conservation Corps of Minnesota and Iowa. I spent summers building and fixing trails, and doing other conservation work around state parks in Minnesota and Iowa. The small work crews included people who were deaf and hard-of-hearing, and I learned American Sign Language during my time with the corps.

My love of learning new languages carried over into my studies at university where I majored in French and Translation at a small college north of New York City. It was important to me to come out of college with a high proficiency in another language, and I spent a little over a year living in Paris.

After I finished university, unsure of what to do with myself, I moved down to New York City. Like so many others who are drawn to New York, I was looking for a certain type of creative life that its myth and history tends to promise. I loved New York — still do — for all its chaos and intensity. I worked a series of odd jobs in archives, libraries, publishing houses, as a dog walker and cat sitter (and many more) before getting trained as a software engineer. The kind of creative life I had imagined in New York exists for some people, surely, but it was not a reality that I could make mine while I was working a full-time job. Towards the end of my time in New York, I felt like I was accumulating art supplies I was too exhausted to use. Neither my partner nor I had ever imagined leaving the city, but a few years after the pandemic hit, we decided to move away for more space, more trees, more fresh air.

Now, I live and work in Florence, MA, USA, a small town about 2 hours west of Boston. I live with my partner, and our dog and two cats. It's a good life, one that's allowed me to reconnect with my natural surroundings and make more art. I still do my best to keep up with my French, and nowadays I have good reading comprehension in Spanish and Italian, too, though my speaking skills are always something I'm working on. What I lack in vocabulary, I usually make up for in enthusiasm and gesticulation :)

Thanks to everyone at Lago Scuro for taking the time to read my application and review my work!
